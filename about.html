OUR STORY.

From quick bites to long catch-ups, The Food Lounge is all about making room for the moments that happen around good food.

Welcome to the lounge

A craving brought you here. Stay for the feeling.

Here in Okara, we believe food is better when it’s shared. A burger after a long day. Shawarma with friends. A pizza at the table when nobody can decide what they want. Those everyday moments are what we’re here for.

Our menu brings together burgers, shawarma, fries, paratha rolls, platters, pasta, pizza, fruit chaat and more. We focus on food made fresh, full of flavour and served with care — whether you choose to sit down with us or order delivery to your door.
